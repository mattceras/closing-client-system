## How to Select a Framework

- **Has case study / named results?** → 5 or 6
- **Lead gen or outbound agency?** → 1 or 24
- **Enrichment data available?** → 4 (most versatile, goes with almost anything)
- **Competing with an incumbent tool?** → 9 or 10
- **Managed / done-for-you service?** → 12
- **AI or automation product?** → 14
- **Creative, video, influencer?** → 8, 16, or 18
- **Can build something in advance?** → 15
- **Seasonal or regulatory hook?** → 19 or 20
- **Website gap spotted?** → 21 or 22
- **Testable / free trial offer?** → 23
- **Data or research to share?** → 7 or 17
- **Pay-per-X pricing model?** → 13
- **Follow-up, no response to Email 1?** → 26, 27, or 28

### Framework 1: AVAERO Core Template
**Best for:** AVAERO's own agency pitches and any lead gen / sales development / outbound agency.
**What it is:** Opens with a specific result, names exact ICP titles and verticals, adds light social proof, then a CTA with risk reversal and a tangible freebie.

```
{FIRST_NAME},

{{random|My team can help you|We can help you|Our team helps companies like yours}} {{random|source|generate|add|book}} [SPECIFIC RESULT — deals, clients, revenue, meetings] {{random|per quarter|per month|every quarter|each month}}

by {{random|connecting you with|getting you in front of|reaching}} [SPECIFIC ICP TITLES] at [SPECIFIC VERTICALS/COMPANY TYPES].

{{random|We recently helped|We just helped|We worked with}} a similar [business type] {{random|that also|who also|that}} [similarity to prospect].

On a {{random|short call|brief call|quick intro}}, I'll {{random|break down|walk you through|show you}} {{random|how we build out prospect lists based on who you're trying to reach|what strategy we use, the results we typically get, and the full approach}} - even if you {{random|end up doing it in-house|decide to handle it yourself|just want to take the strategy and run with it}}. {{random|We'll also put together|We'll also send over|Plus we'll send}} a list of [NUMBER] targeted contacts that fit what you're looking for just for {{random|hopping on|the time|the call}}.
```

**Key variables:** specific result, ICP titles, verticals, similar company reference, contact list number

### Framework 2: Event / Educational Invite
**Best for:** Product companies with demo-able solutions, technical offerings.
**What it is:** Invites prospect to a short educational session with an incentive. Asks for attendance, not a sale.

```
{FIRST_NAME} – {{random|projects ramping up?|got upcoming builds in motion?|gearing up for new projects?}}

We're hosting a {{random|30-min|25-min|quick}} {{random|Lunch & Learn|live demo|webinar}} on [PRODUCT], covering {{random|updated specs, install steps, cost math, and new case studies|everything from specs and install tips to ROI math and recent projects}} so you can {{random|streamline upcoming builds|move faster on new jobs|stay ahead on project planning}}.

{{random|Lunch is on us.|We'll send lunch to your team.|Food's covered, on us.}}

{{random|Does next Wednesday or Thursday afternoon suit your team?|Would next Wed or Thu afternoon work for you?}}

{{random|Thanks|Best|All the best|Cheers}},
{{random|Sender|Sender L.|Full Name}}
```

### Framework 3: Stat-Led Pain Opener
**Best for:** Any offer where the pain is quantifiable.
**What it is:** Opens with a quantified pain point, positions the solution as the fix.

```
{FIRST_NAME}, [SPECIFIC PAIN STAT about their role/industry].

{{random|Our|The}} [PRODUCT/SERVICE] {{random|handles|manages|takes care of}} [what it does to solve that] — [brief proof or mechanism].

{{random|Worth a quick look?|Want to see how it works?|Open to exploring this?}}

{{random|Thanks|Best|All the best|Cheers}},
{{random|Sender|Sender L.|Full Name}}
```

**Strong pain stat examples:**
- "most eCommerce support teams spend 60%+ of their time on the same five questions"
- "labor costs are rising across women's health practices"
- "aluminum CBAM rates are hitting harder than steel"

### Framework 4: Personalized Trigger (Enrichment-Driven)
**Best for:** Any industry when enrichment data is available. The go-to versatile framework.
**What it is:** Uses enrichment data (company description, keywords, services) to write a personalized opener connecting their business to the offer.

```
{FIRST_NAME} - {PERSONALIZATION}

{{random|Have you considered|Ever thought about|Curious if you've explored}} [how client's offer connects to what they do]?

{{random|We developed|Our team built|We built}} [solution description] that [key benefit].

{{random|Thanks|Best|All the best|Cheers}},
{{random|Sender|Sender L.|Full Name}}
```

**Note:** The `{PERSONALIZATION}` variable is generated from the enrichment column — a 1-sentence line about what the company does and why the offer is relevant.

### Framework 6: Anonymized "Similar Company" Case Study
**Best for:** When no name-droppable brands exist, or confidentiality matters.
**What it is:** Describes a result for a similar company without naming them.

```
{FIRST_NAME} – {{random|we recently helped|we just helped|we worked with}} [similar company descriptor] that {{random|doubled|grew|scaled}} [metric] to [number] while {{random|maintaining|keeping|holding}} [quality metric].

{{random|Would you like to learn how?|Want to see how we did it?|Interested in how we made it happen?}}

{{random|Sender|Sender L.|Full Name}}
```

**Strong descriptors:** "an HR tech startup" / "a 300-unit luxury apartment complex" / "one aluminum extruder, 180 employees" / "a company in your space"

### Framework 9: Competitive Displacement / Contract Buyout
**Best for:** SaaS replacement, agency switches, vendor changes.
**What it is:** Targets prospects using a specific competitor, offers to replace with migration/buyout sweetener.
**Requires:** Tech stack enrichment data or industry knowledge of dominant tools.

```
{FIRST_NAME},

{{random|I know|I understand}} [COMPETITOR]'s {{random|premium pricing and ongoing consulting fees|high costs and complexity}} can {{random|seriously drain|take a big hit on}} your [budget type] ({{random|we hear this all the time|this is a common concern}}).

{{random|Thanks|Best|Cheers}},
{{random|Sender|Sender L.|Full Name}}
```

### Framework 26: "Right Person?" Thread Reply
**Best for:** Universal follow-up on any Email 1.
**What it is:** Ultra-short routing question. Under 25 words. Always a thread reply.

```
Am I {{random|reaching out to|contacting|getting in touch with}} the right person to {{random|chat|talk|discuss}} about [TOPIC] {{random|at|for}} {COMPANY}, {FIRST_NAME}?

- {{random|Sender|Sender L.|Full Name}}
```

**Variation — with mini context:**
```
{{random|Quick check|Just checking|Wanted to confirm}} - are you the {{random|right|best}} person to {{random|handle|own|manage}} [SPECIFIC FUNCTION] for {COMPANY}, {FIRST_NAME}?

- {{random|Sender|Sender L.|Full Name}}
```

### Framework 27: Warm Follow-Up / Float Back Up
**Best for:** Follow-up when Email 1 got no response. Thread reply preferred.
**What it is:** Gentle nudge. Acknowledges they're busy. Zero pressure.

```
{{random|Floating this back up|Just following up}}, {FIRST_NAME}.

{{random|If [topic] isn't a priority right now, no worries.|If this isn't on your radar right now, totally understand.}} {{random|But if it is|If it's worth exploring}}, {{random|happy to show you what we built|can show you how this works}} for [similar company/use case].

{{random|Either way|No pressure}},
{{random|Sender|Sender L.|Full Name}}
```

**Variation — new angle:**
```
{FIRST_NAME}, {{random|thought you might be interested|quick note}}:

We recently {{random|completed a project|worked with a company}} similar to what {COMPANY} might be {{random|planning|working on}}, where the client [SPECIFIC DIFFERENT RESULT than Email 1].

{{random|Would you like to hear about how they approached it?|Want to see how they did it?}}

{{random|Thanks|Best|Cheers}},
{{random|Sender|Sender L.|Full Name}}
```

## Principles from Top Performers

1. **Specificity wins.** "$200k/month from $0 in 90 days" beats "significant revenue growth."
2. **CTA must be binary.** "Worth a quick look?" not "What are your thoughts?"
3. **Social proof > claims.** Don't say "we're the best." Say "we did Y for Z."
4. **Short > long.** Highest performers almost all under 50 words.
5. **3-4 spintax variants** per bracket for deliverability.
6. **Domain obfuscation** in signatures: `company(dot)com`
7. **P.S. lines** work for proof stats that would clutter the body.
8. **"Even if you do it in-house"** is a top CTA pattern.
9. **Name specific ICP titles** — not "decision makers" but "VPs of Marketing at mid-market SaaS."
10. **Multiple case studies > one.** Drop 2 names with results when possible.
