# Worked Examples

These are fully scored example offers used to calibrate the rubric. Reference these when scoring is ambiguous — find the closest analog and reason from there.

---

## Example 1: Performance-Based Cold Email Lead Generation

**Offer:** $500-$1,000/mo tech fee plus $200-$400 per booked showed-up call. No case studies.

- **New Money: 4/4** — Pure new leads from cold outbound.
- **Done-For-You: 4/4** — Full management of cold email infrastructure.
- **Derisked: 3/4** — Performance pricing (strong) but no case studies yet.
- **Low Commitment: 2/4** — Low fixed fee, pay-per-result structure helps, but still recurring.
- **Total: 13/16 — Works on cold.**

## Example 2: Done-For-You Cold Calling

**Offer:** $18,000 per quarter. 1,000 dials per day. Some case studies showing calls booked but no revenue quantification. No guarantee.

- **New Money: 4/4** — Net-new outbound calls to cold leads.
- **Done-For-You: 4/4** — Fully managed cold calling operation.
- **Derisked: 2/4** — Case studies show activity (calls booked) but not ROI (clients signed). No guarantee.
- **Low Commitment: 1/4** — $18k per quarter is heavy.
- **Total: 11/16 — Works on cold with optimization.** Needs better case studies or a guarantee tied to booked calls.

## Example 3: AI Assisted Agency Program

**Offer:** Six month coaching program for $12,000. Coaching calls, course, and community. Hundreds of case studies. 60-day money-back guarantee.

- **New Money: 4/4** — Students build new agencies generating new revenue.
- **Done-For-You: 1/4** — It's a coaching program; students execute.
- **Derisked: 3/4** — 60-day money-back guarantee + massive volume of case studies.
- **Low Commitment: 1/4** — $12k is high commitment for a coaching program.
- **Total: 9/16 — This is a warm traffic offer.** It works on warm because of trust/content, not cold metrics. Don't try to run this offer on cold ads directly — build a front-end first.

- **New Money: 4/4** — Students scale with ads, generating new revenue.

## Example 9: AI Receptionist for Medspas

**Offer:** $497 setup + $297/month. Guarantee: "30+ extra appointments in 60 days or you don't pay a dollar." Named case studies with specific numbers. One regional chain case study.

- **New Money: 2/4** — Calls were already coming in; this is scalping with "new appointments" framing. Word trickery works to sell but scoring is honest.
- **Done-For-You: 4/4** — AI handles calls, full setup included.
- **Derisked: 4/4** — Real outcome-based money-back guarantee + specific case studies with quantified results + one semi-big name.
- **Low Commitment: 3/4** — $297/mo is cheap, $497 setup is minimal, but still recurring.
- **Total: 13/16 — Works on cold.** Strong example of how 3 cranked dials compensate for a capped dial.

## Patterns to recognize

**Courses and coaching programs almost always cap at DFY 1-2/4.** To make them work on cold, you either need a low-ticket version (front-end) or a hybrid with a real DFY element. Most coaching programs are warm traffic offers.

**Ad spend requirements structurally cap Low Commitment at 2.5/4.** Nothing you can do positioning-wise changes this.

**Case studies without ROI numbers are weak derisking.** "Calls booked" is activity; "clients signed" is ROI. Activity proves the service works mechanically, not that it generates money.

**The compensation principle works at the total level, not per-dial.** A 2/4 New Money with 4/4/4/3 on the others = 13/16 and works on cold. Don't artificially inflate a weak dial because other dials are strong.

**"This is a warm traffic offer" is a legitimate verdict.** Many offers are well-designed for their purpose (warm traffic) but will never work on cold. Tell the user this directly rather than trying to force changes that would break the warm version.
