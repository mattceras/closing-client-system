# Cold-Traffic Offer Check

Every lead magnet idea the skill proposes MUST pass this check. If ANY item fails, rewrite the idea before proposing it.

This is baked into the skill so it works without depending on any other skill installed on the user's machine.

---

## PASS / FAIL

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| **1. New money** | Creates NEW revenue (new customers, new jobs, new deals). NOT optimisation, audit, recovery, cost-cutting, efficiency, or reactivation. |
| **2. 3-second stranger test** | A stranger reading the magnet name understands what they'll get in under 3 seconds. No jargon. No categories inside categories. |
| **3. Category slot** | Sits inside ONE of: Ads / Funnels / Content / Lead Gen / Strategy. Nothing outside. |
| **3b. No cannibalisation** | The magnet category MUST NOT be the same as the client's own core service. If the client sells Content, don't propose a "Content Calendar Preview" — it looks like a sample of their service, not a sneak peek. Rewrite the magnet into a different category. |
| **4. AI-fulfillable** | The mock-up itself can be produced by a solo operator with AI tools (ChatGPT image gen, Claude, context.dev). No human agency required to produce the preview. |
| **5. Preview language** | Named "Preview", "Mock-Up", "Guide", "Blueprint", "Board", "Kit". NEVER "Audit". NEVER "Analysis". NEVER "Review". |
| **6. Deliverable is visual** | The magnet is a THING you can send as an image. Not a PDF, not a call, not a document. An image. |

## HARD BANS

- "Audit" — signals work, not value. Ban.
- "Analysis" — signals homework, not value. Ban.
- "Review" — signals judgement, not value. Ban.
- "Assessment" — signals bureaucracy. Ban.
- "Consultation" — implies you talk them into buying. Ban.
- "Strategy session" — every strategy session smells like a pitch. Ban.
- Optimisation framing ("improve", "streamline", "reduce waste", "cut costs") — belongs on warm traffic, not cold. Ban for the magnet NAME. Body text of emails can reference these if the magnet itself is new-money-shaped.

## GOOD EXAMPLES (pass all 6)

- **In-Store Campaign Preview** (retail POS execution — physical mock, Lead Gen)
- **Pipeline Preview** (outbound / SDR — dashboard mock, Lead Gen)
- **Custom Gift Guide** (merch / promo, Content)
- **Dream Client Blueprint** (lead gen agency, Lead Gen)
- **Content Calendar Mock-Up** (agency serving creators, Content — but NOT if the client is themselves a content agency, see check 3b)
- **Ad Creative Board** (paid media / ads, Ads)
- **Positioning Preview** (consultancy — one-page positioning canvas, Strategy)
- **GTM Motion Preview** (RevOps / GTM consulting — motion diagram, Strategy)
- **Repositioning Blueprint** (consultancy — before/after positioning, Strategy)

## BAD EXAMPLES (fail at least one check)

- **Retail POS Spend Audit** — fails 1 (optimisation not new money), 5 (audit language)
- **Enterprise Team Access Review** — fails 1, 2, 5, and 6
- **Free Growth Consultation** — fails 5, 6
- **Marketing Health Check** — fails 1 (optimisation), 5

## Generic vs Per-prospect classification

Every passing magnet must ALSO be classified:

### Generic
One image works across all prospects. The mock-up doesn't need the prospect's logo or brand colours to land.

**Examples:** Pipeline Preview (SDR mock), Content Calendar Mock-Up (creator services), Dream Client Blueprint (lead gen).

**Scale:** operator builds the image once, uses it across their whole outbound list.

### Per-prospect
The mock-up needs the specific prospect's logo, brand colours, or context to land. Different image for every lead.

**Examples:** In-Store Campaign Preview (needs B&M logo on the store window), Custom Gift Guide (needs the prospect's brand on the merch), Ad Creative Board (needs the prospect's product visible).

**Scale:** operator uses context.dev to pull each prospect's logo from URL, then re-runs the ChatGPT prompt with the logo swap. Or, only generates the image after a prospect replies YES to email 1 (manual, one at a time).

Every proposed magnet has ONE tag: Generic or Per-prospect. Never both. Never neither.

## When to propose BOTH Generic AND Per-prospect variants

Default: pick the best fit and tag it. BUT — for these client types, propose BOTH variants of at least ONE magnet, side by side, so the operator picks the volume vs. impact trade-off:

- **Solo consultants / personal brands.** A per-prospect canvas filled in for the specific prospect (e.g. Vercel's positioning canvas) lands 3x harder than a generic template. But the operator sends fewer emails. Show both.
- **Low-volume, high-value outbound** (targeting 20-50 dream clients, not 1,000). Per-prospect wins on impact, Generic on speed. Show both.
- **Any time the client has stated they only need 3-5 clients this year.** Per-prospect only. Skip Generic entirely.

For BPD-scale operators sending 1,000+ emails/week, Generic is usually the right default unless the client's category demands per-prospect (like BPD retail POS).
