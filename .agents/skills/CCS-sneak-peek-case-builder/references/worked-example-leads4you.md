## What could go wrong

- **The dashboard mock must look real.** Lorem ipsum names or fake numbers = "these people don't actually run outbound at scale". Every row plausible.
- **First-name-only founder problem.** Directive's founder will diligence Kristian. If no LinkedIn presence, meeting gets pushed. Have Kristian's founder credibility asset ready.
- **Category self-cannibalisation.** Directive already runs outbound-adjacent work. Angle must lead with SDR-desk-as-service, not outbound-as-service.
