# Your Built-In CCS Coach

The Closing Client System is designed to teach you how to operate cold outbound, not make you permanently dependent on a coach.

It uses three layers:

1. **Your context:** your private agency profile, offers, client profiles, campaign notes, results, and coaching progress.
2. **CCS judgment:** the offer, targeting, list-building, copy, operations, reply, and sales frameworks in this repository.
3. **Execution:** specialist CCS agents that complete the work or guide you through it.

## Questions you can ask

- **What can you do?** — shows what is ready now, what needs a connection, and what can optionally be automated in the cloud.
- **What should I do next?** — looks for the narrowest current bottleneck and gives you the next one to three actions.
- **Based on all my data, what do you recommend?** — reviews the information CCS can actually access and recommends the highest-leverage move.
- **Based on my offer, what would you recommend?** — evaluates whether the offer works for cold traffic and connects it to an ICP, wedge, signal, angle, and minimum viable test.
- **Coach me through this** — completes the work with you while teaching the important decision rules.
- **Teach me how to do this** — puts more emphasis on explanation and practice.
- **Am I ready to do this myself?** — gives an evidence-based independence assessment for the part of CCS you use.

You can ask normal follow-up questions. You do not need to know skill names, file names, or technical commands.

## How coaching changes over time

CCS starts with more explanation and gradually shifts through four modes:

- **Guided:** one stage at a time, with unfamiliar terms explained.
- **Collaborative:** CCS recommends the move and handles the mechanical work while you make key decisions.
- **On demand:** you operate routine campaigns; CCS acts as an analyst and second set of eyes.
- **Graduated:** routine work no longer needs coaching; CCS focuses on audits, experiments, and unusual cases.

Progress is based on completed real work, not a quiz or a confidence score. Graduation applies to a defined scope. For example, someone may be independent at running PlusVibe campaigns while still needing guided help with cloud automation.

## What CCS remembers

The private `coaching-progress.md` file records the current goal, demonstrated capabilities, remaining gaps, and a short evidence log. It stays with the student and is excluded from GitHub product updates.

CCS never treats a missing file, disconnected account, or unavailable report as evidence. If information cannot be inspected, it labels that information `UNKNOWN` instead of pretending.

## When a human is still needed

The built-in coach should answer repository and workflow questions itself. Human support is reserved for account ownership or billing, inaccessible permissions, product bugs, legal or regulatory questions outside the included materials, and business facts only the human coach can know.

When support is genuinely needed, CCS prepares a concise non-sensitive support summary so the student does not have to reconstruct everything from scratch.
